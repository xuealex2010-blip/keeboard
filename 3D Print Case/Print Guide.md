Make sure that the parts are oriented correctly, this should only apply to the bars. 

They should be oriented properly at the start, look for the teardrop shapes, face them upwards to reduce support material.

For big parts, make sure to use a brim to keep bed adhesion, and prevent warping.


Make sure there are 2 side bars.

All of the parts should consist of:

Bar Back

Bar Front

Bar Sides - x2

Bottom Cover Left

Bottom Cover Right

Plate Left

Plate Right

Knob
